What Is PostureGuard?
PostureGuard is a wearable electronic device designed to help students and developers become more aware of their sitting posture and remember to take movement breaks during long periods of studying or working.
The device uses a motion sensor to monitor changes in body orientation and provides gentle vibration reminders when configured conditions are met.

How to Use It
Wear the device in its designated position.
Turn it on and complete the initial calibration.
Continue with your normal studying or working activities.
The device monitors motion and checks for sustained changes in orientation.
Receive a gentle vibration reminder when the configured conditions are met.
Receive separate reminders to take movement breaks.
The usage instructions describe the planned operation; they will be updated after the device is built and tested.

Why I Made PostureGuard
I developed PostureGuard because I noticed that students and developers can spend long hours studying or working in front of computers without paying attention to their sitting habits or remembering to take movement breaks.
I wanted to explore how embedded systems and wearable technology could help address this problem through a simple device that provides timely reminders.
My goal is to create a practical, comfortable and accessible solution that encourages better awareness of posture and regular movement during long working sessions.
